Vertex Launcher privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

Vertex Launcher is a home screen for Android. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not sell or share your information. Almost everything it reads stays on your phone. The one exception is your approximate location, which is sent to a weather service so the weather tile can show your local forecast.

Who we are

Vertex Launcher is developed by Judd Edwards. If you have a question about this policy, contact judd.edwards@gmail.com.

What the app reads on your phone

A home screen needs to see some of what is on your phone to do its job. Everything in this table is read and stored only on your device.

WhatWhy
Installed appsTo list, search, open, add to home and uninstall your apps. Android requires launchers to request visibility of all installed apps for this.
How often you open appsTo show your frequently used apps first. Only a launch count and the time of the last launch are kept.
Your home screenYour pages, tiles, folders, widgets, shortcuts and settings.
Calendar (optional)To show upcoming events on the calendar and today tiles. Event titles, times and locations are read but never stored or sent anywhere.
Notifications (optional)To show unread counts on tiles and in the app list, the latest notifications on the notifications tile, and what is playing on the music tile. Notification contents are only held in memory while shown.
Approximate location (optional)To show local weather. Vertex only asks for approximate location, never precise location.

The optional items are off until you allow them, and you can turn each one off again in your phone's settings at any time. Vertex works without them; the related tile just asks for access instead.

What leaves your phone

  • Weather. If you allow location, Vertex sends your approximate coordinates, rounded to about 100 metres, over an encrypted connection to Open-Meteo to fetch the forecast. Nothing else is sent with it, and no identifier for you or your device is attached. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy describes how it handles requests.
  • Place name. To label the weather with your town, Vertex uses Android's built-in location lookup. That lookup is provided by your phone's system, which on most phones is run by Google, under the system's own terms.
  • Searches. If you search the web or the app store from the app list, Vertex hands the search to your browser or store app. Those apps handle it under their own policies; Vertex does not see or keep the results.

Vertex does not send anything else off your phone. We do not run a server, and we receive no data from the app.

Backups

If you have Android backup turned on, your phone may include Vertex's home screen layout and settings in your own device backup, stored in your Google account. We cannot access that backup.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information.
  • No access to precise location, contacts, photos, messages or files.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Turn off calendar, notification or location access at any time in Android's settings. Vertex stops reading that information straight away.
  • Clearing Vertex's storage in Android's app settings, or uninstalling it, deletes everything it has stored on your phone.
  • Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Vertex Launcher is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of information changes, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
